— 01 / OUR STORY

Global Indians deserve a financial life that moves with them.

Living across borders shouldn’t mean navigating a fragmented financial life back home. We’re building a simpler way for global Indians to invest, manage money and make important financial decisions in India, wherever they live.

2020

Mudit Vijayvergiya founded SBNRI in 2020 to make investing and essential financial services in India simpler for NRIs. What should have been straightforward often meant fragmented information, paperwork, and too many handoffs.

SBNRI began by building around the global Indian, not treating them as an exception like traditional banking systems do. That idea shapes the work: make the complicated understandable, make access easier, and earn trust in every interaction.

Mudit VijayvergiyaFounder & CEO
Ayush NalotiaCo-founder & CPO

From that founding vision, the SBNRI team has grown into a diverse group of product, technology, operations, and financial experts who share one mission: make investing and essential financial services in India simpler for NRIs.

Today, every team member carries that same intent forward: simplify the complicated, make access easier, and earn trust in every interaction, so the global Indian can move forward with confidence.
